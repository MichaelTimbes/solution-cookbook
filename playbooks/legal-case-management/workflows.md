# Legal Case Management Workflows

## 1) Matter Intake and Classification

1. Legal request is submitted.
2. Jurisdiction, matter type, and priority are classified.
3. Matter owner and team are assigned.

## 2) Evidence and Review Progression

1. Evidence links are attached and validated.
2. Review tasks are routed to legal roles.
3. Approvals or revisions update matter state.

Capabilities:
- [Approval Workflows / Human-In-The-Loop](../../cookbook-v1/capabilities/approval-workflows-human-in-the-loop.md)
- [Audit Log + Provenance](../../cookbook-v1/capabilities/audit-log-provenance.md)

## 3) Filing and Closure

1. Filing artifacts are prepared.
2. External submission status is recorded.
3. Matter closure captures final disposition.
