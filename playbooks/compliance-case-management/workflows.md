# Compliance Case Management Workflows

## 1) Case Intake and Policy Mapping

1. Exception or alert enters intake.
2. Policy and control mappings are applied.
3. Case severity and ownership are assigned.

## 2) Investigation and Evidence Collection

1. Investigation tasks are created.
2. Evidence is linked and validated.
3. Findings are documented.

## 3) Remediation and Approval

1. Remediation plan is executed.
2. Review and approval gates validate closure criteria.
3. Case status transitions to closed.
