# Business Process Automation Data Model

- Core: `ProcessDefinition`, `ProcessVersion`, `Instance`, `Task`
- Metadata: `DecisionInput`, `DecisionOutcome`
- Governance: `OverrideAction`, `AuditEvent`
- Workflow: `TransitionRecord`, `TimerEvent`, `CompensationRecord`
- Integration: `IntegrationTask`, `RetryAttempt`, `DeadLetterRecord`
