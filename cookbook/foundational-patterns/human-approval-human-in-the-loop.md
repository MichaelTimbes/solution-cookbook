# Human Approval / Human-In-The-Loop

## Description of the pattern
Human-In-The-Loop introduces explicit human decision points in automated workflows for risk control, governance, and accountability.

## Why it appears across enterprise systems
Not all decisions are safe to fully automate. High-impact actions often require explicit approval, delegation, or override.

## Common failure modes when ignored
- Unauthorized high-risk actions
- Unclear accountability for exceptions
- Manual escalations happening outside system control
